Six steps for one field

Since 1 August 2025, coated seed in Quebec needs an agronomist's prescription. A bait-trap count shows whether your field needs it.

  1. Your field. Type its position or use this device's.
  2. Trap week. The week the soil reaches 12 °C, from NASA data.
  3. Where to trap. Ten trap points on the 3D field.
  4. Enter counts. Wireworms per trap, against the threshold.
  5. Photo and notes. Log a soil insect, standing water, ground cover or a water channel on the field.
  6. What to plant. Four rotations over five years: soil, water, nitrogen and pests.
